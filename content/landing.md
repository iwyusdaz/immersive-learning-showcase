## The principle

Each project follows one principle: **realism where it helps learning, restraint where it doesn't.**
Realistic detail is used where noticing it *is* the learning — terrain relief under low sun, wear marks on an artifact, the visual cue of a spill — and everything that competes for attention during a task is simplified. Cinematics are short, captioned, skippable from the first frame, and never play during assessment. The design rationale, a realism matrix and a shot list are documented in every repo.

Built with three.js, real open data (NASA, USGS, Smithsonian CC0), and CC0 assets. Learning content is CC BY 4.0; code is MIT.
